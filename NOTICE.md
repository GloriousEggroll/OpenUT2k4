## No game data included

OpenUT2k4 contains no Unreal Tournament 2004 content: no maps, textures, models, sounds, music or text from
the original game. To play, you need your own copy of UT2004. The game converts that copy on your computer
the first time it starts, and the converted files stay on your computer. If you choose "Download UT2004 from
OldUnreal", the game downloads the installer image from OldUnreal's servers to your computer; the Epic Games
Terms of Service (https://legal.epicgames.com/en-US/epicgames/tos) apply to that download and to the game data.
